# Sage Harvest Seed Supply Chain Diagnostic

An interactive strategic maturity diagnostic for commercial seed supply chains, turning operational experience into actionable supply-chain intelligence.

## What it assesses

The diagnostic covers 10 dimensions and 30 questions across:
- Demand & Market Planning
- Seed Production Planning
- Procurement & Parent Seed
- Quality Management
- Processing & Capacity
- Inventory & Working Capital
- Traceability & Data
- Decision Intelligence
- Resilience & Risk
- Sustainability & Carbon

It provides an indicative maturity profile, dimension scores, strategic observations, priority gaps and recommended moves.

## Scope

The diagnostic is designed for strategic reflection and does not require confidential company information. Results are indicative and should not be treated as an audit or certification.

## Development principle

The tool is maintained as an independent digital product so that its scoring model, experience and future intelligence features can evolve without destabilising the Sage Harvest corporate website.
